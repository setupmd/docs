While we cannot make a purchase decision for you, if you selected from one of the hosting providers in our list here's what you can expect to happen next.

### Purchase confirmation

Ensure you see an active service in your billing account and an email confirming your purchase. If you do not see this, check your spam folder or contact the provider's support team. In rare instances you may hit a fraud prevention measure in which case your service will not automatically be deployed and be stuck in a pending state.

The Wiki cannot assist with these issues and you need to raise a ticket with the respective support team.

### Game panel invite

Within 5-10 minutes you should receive an email from the provider with an invite to their game panel. This is where you will manage your server, including starting, stopping, and configuring it. All of the providers in our list utilise a variant of the Pterodactyl panel and you must set a unique password before attempting to login.

Your username will typically be your email address and the password **DOES NOT SYNC** with your billing account.
